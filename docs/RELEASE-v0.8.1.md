# SIRA Platform v0.8.1

## Task card update

- The entire task card is a direct link to `/tasks/[id]`.
- Drag and drop is available through the dedicated handle on the card.
- `Edito` remains a separate action for editing the task's basic data.
- The card now focuses on title, linked project, client/person, colored labels and due date.

No new database migration is required after v0.8.0.
